This work develops an analytical theory for random monitored Clifford circuits subject to weak non-Clifford perturbations. It establishes existence and uniqueness of the stationary distribution and determines the leading steady-state magic scaling in the weak-injection regime. Gross--Wigner mana scales linearly in odd-prime local dimensions, whereas qubit 2-stabilizer Rényi entropy has quadratic scaling. The contrast is traced to the different local geometries of these resource measures near stabilizer states.
